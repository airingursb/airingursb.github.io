# Panda identity bible

- Exactly one moderately plump young giant panda, seated in a stable three-quarter body pose.
- Round fluffy head, small rounded charcoal ears, cream muzzle, small glossy black oval nose.
- Small oval eyes inside soft charcoal eye patches; pupils remain distinct and movable.
- Charcoal forelegs and visible rear haunch, warm ivory-white head and torso, tiny white tail.
- Calm shy closed-mouth smile, relaxed short eyebrows, pale peach cheek blush.
- Delicate children's-book watercolor texture with sparse broken charcoal ink lines.
- Body, paws, rear haunch, tail, scale, bottom contact point, camera, framing, and lighting never move.
- Only head yaw, head pitch, and pupil direction may change. No blinking, breathing, idle sway, or secondary motion.
