Airing to Chuqing

18 Jul 2026

Hello, Chuqing

Thank you for writing and sharing what you are going through.

Having read your words, I would say from my own experience that none of these three things is a particularly big problem. It is good that you have noticed them at an early stage, but you need not be excessively anxious.

First, grades. If you are not planning postgraduate or further study, grades have less influence on your future. The most important thing is to be clear about what you want to achieve during your four years at university. If you are aiming for postgraduate study, work toward becoming an excellent student; if you want to study abroad, start preparing your English early; if you want a good job, try to do projects, enter competitions and win awards. Or, if you have no particular goal yet, cherish each day and use your limited time to discover what you love and what you are good at.

Second, procrastination. I procrastinate too, so the saying that deadlines are the best motivator is not without reason. If you have carried a task in your head since the first day of the break, or filled a to-do app with things you think about every day without doing them, you might be better off planning your break sensibly. For a long time, I have spent Saturdays doing very little and only started studying on Sunday afternoons. On the day off, I do not think about work or what remains undone. I put it all out of my mind and enjoy the present. Only by living each particular moment well can we avoid anxiety. And if we finish before the deadline, it is not really procrastination, is it?

[The response to private relationship matters unrelated to this issue has been removed.]

Also, when someone feels low, starting something that requires a long commitment and offers no visible result in the short term can be harder. So I would not recommend simply concluding that your current state means you have poor willpower. The more you judge and explain yourself in those terms, the easier it can be to enter a downward cycle of anxiety, avoidance and self-blame. Give yourself a few days to recover your mood and energy, then gather what you need, make a plan and set off again.

Wishing you a smooth journey.

Airing
