NO. 15Reply

Airing to Zhaoye

24 Jul 2025

〔Zhaoye〕:

Hello. I will offer some views on your two concerns from my own perspective, for reference.

Of the two choices you describe, one seems to me to arise from longing for something you love, and the other from settling for what would be enough. Life is short. I would suggest using the advantage of being young to pursue what you love without leaving yourself regrets.

If you really like computer vision, that enthusiasm and curiosity should lead you to explore it more deeply on your own. You can look for and pursue research projects and related competitions in that direction, and papers will naturally emerge from projects. True passion is fearless. That also addresses your second concern.

Right now, what you may most need is to quietly ask yourself what you actually want and what you are trying to pursue: a scientific field you love, or a Project 211 credential that looks good to others; academic inquiry, or skills development. Once you have your own answer, the road will open before you.

I hope the road ahead becomes clear.

Airing
