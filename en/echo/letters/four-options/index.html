Airing to Zhiqiu

4 Apr 2026

Personally, I would suggest D over A.

I do not think D’s two disadvantages amount to much.

1. The depth of the project and the experience it offers would be competitive enough if you wanted to return to a large company.

2. Whether overtime is worth a [size of increase removed] pay rise is up to you. I think it is acceptable soon after graduation. If you would otherwise use your free time to develop yourself, and the work at [company D] has enough depth to teach you something, I think the trade is worthwhile.

I would not recommend considering B.

Staying at A is also a better short-term choice, but not the best long-term one. Since you have been promised a strong midyear performance rating, I do not think you need to rush these next few months. You could wait and see, and ideally try to secure a quick promotion along the way. I think leaving at [level removed] would offer the best value.

Airing
