Tinglan to Airing

Date not recorded

The date of this individual message was not recorded; its correspondence record is dated 2025-04-17.

Hi, I am 〔Tinglan〕. I happened to come across your annual review, “2024, Farewell to Midsummer.” Reading it closely, I felt a strong sense of recognition, so I wanted to send you this informal message as an ordinary reader.

For various reasons, I too began using words to record my emotions and state of mind this year. Only after developing this habit did I realize that however hard we try to find value and meaning in the present moment, it is like walking through yellow sand: we cannot see, and receive no clear response. People really do begin to understand themselves when they look back. I am grateful for writing: it is a good tool for repeatedly getting to know myself. And I am grateful for your writing, which gives me a calm kind of strength. Sweeping emotions may attract attention, but calm strength is the best state in which to face a thunderclap or receive congratulations.

I also shared the article with friends. Our lives have felt rather robotic lately, and I thought your article and your words might stir their numb hearts and restore a little enthusiasm for life.

By the way, I use a diary app to keep these records and recommend it to you too. I wonder whether it resembles the project you are making. As a way of recording moods, it has genuinely eased some of the anxiety and unease that have spilled out of me.
