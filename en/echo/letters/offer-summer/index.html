1. Backend development at [cloud services company and business removed], [compensation and city removed].

2. Software development in test at [mobility company and department removed], [estimated compensation and city removed].

1. Concern about the future prospects of test development.

2. Concern that the shared quality platform is peripheral to the business.

1. A stronger name on my résumé, making future job changes easier.

NO. 07Reply

Airing to Xingzhou

20 Jun 2025

Xingzhou,

Thank you for your trust and the update. I do not think either option is particularly ideal, but time is also becoming a real constraint.

Your analysis of [mobility company] is fairly thorough, and I also think it may be better than [cloud services company]. When you interned there in test development, were there more opportunities to do development work? You could also ask someone inside. And ask whether anyone has successfully transferred internally from test development to a development role.

If both of those points check out, I do not think the problem is too great. But plan your career path after joining. There are probably two routes:

1. Progress quickly within the testing role.

2. Build experience quickly, then transfer internally or change companies to move into development.

Plan the timing of those steps.

Best wishes,

Airing
